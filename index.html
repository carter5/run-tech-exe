01 / CAREER TALKS

11 AM SESSION BLOCK · CENTRAL

Dear Rejection Email: Thank You, Next — How AI Changed My Job Search Forever

Shaily Goyal ↗

This session explores how AI can transform the job search process, from resume optimization and personalized applications to interview preparation and identifying career opportunities. Through my personal experiences, I’ll share how leveraging AI tools can make job searching more strategic, efficient, and personalized, while emphasizing the importance of authenticity, continuous learning, and resilience in navigating rejection and building a successful career.

1 PM SESSION BLOCK · CENTRAL

Standing Out When Everyone Has the Same Tools: A 45-minute career playbook for early-career tech in the age of AI

Bri Flynn, Ph.D. ↗

Everyone on your team now has the same AI tools, and the tasks that used to train new engineers, analysts, and designers are the ones AI does first. So how do you stand out in your first few years? In this interactive session, we’ll look at why the early-career market is changing shape (from pyramid to diamond), why AI access is equal but AI leverage is not, and where human judgment still sets the price. You’ll practice that judgment in a live exercise. Then you’ll score yourself on the Four C’s (Career, Capabilities, Capacity, and Character) and leave with one specific, dated, accountable action for the next 30 days.

02 / TECH TALKS

11 AM SESSION BLOCK · CENTRAL

Building CropMerge: Turning Drone Imagery into Practical Farm Insights

Kash Setty ↗&Robbie Harris ↗

CropMerge is building software that helps growers use drone imagery within their existing scouting workflows. I’ll walk through our RGB image-processing approach, including preserving source photographs and producing source-linked reports for human review. The session will explore how farmer feedback and field validation help us assess which observations could be useful, and where the limits of image-based analysis matter.

12 PM SESSION BLOCK · CENTRAL

The Hard Part Isn’t the Agent: Making Business Knowledge Accessible with Context and Control

Shreyas Venkatarathinam ↗

Business users need answers, not another dashboard built for a specific question or a SQL extract requiring further manual analysis in Excel. This session explores how domain expertise, data engineering, and well-defined business rules can transform complex enterprise data into trusted, accessible information—and how purpose-built AI agents can democratize business analytics without sacrificing accuracy or control. Through practical examples, attendees will learn how high-quality datasets, business context, and appropriate guardrails enable reliable, explainable AI solutions that support better business decisions.

2 PM SESSION BLOCK · CENTRAL

From Conversation to Code - Imagining an AI-native product workflow for enterprises

Jack Rosenthal ↗&Sourabh Daroji ↗

The biggest drag on product teams isn’t execution, it’s unclear direction. By weaving AI into early product conversations, we were able to transform loosely defined product requirements/ideas into structured artifacts and rapid prototypes that brought clarity and alignment quickly between product management, design and engineering. Pairing this with AI-supported engineering handoff created a smoother, more consistent path from design intent to implementation.

4 PM SESSION BLOCK · CENTRAL

Healthcare AI Is a Systems Problem. Let's Talk About the System

Sai Jahnavi ↗

Getting into healthcare data science is more difficult than most industries and this is not because tech skills are different, but because the domain is unlike anything else. And nobody really teaches you that part. That’s what this talk is for. The U.S. spends $4.5 trillion a year on healthcare, and yet doctors spend more time charting than caring for patients. Hospitals lose millions of dollars due to preventable readmissions and coding errors, most of which are hidden in claims data. The data is already there. What’s needed is context like where it comes from, why it looks like it does, and how to model it so it actually works in the real world.

We will take a patient journey from the point of hospitalization to data, billing, and finally to a modeling task. We will then discuss where AI adds value and how to build responsibly when real patients are involved. This talk is for data scientists and analysts interested in healthcare but feel like they are missing the bigger picture.
